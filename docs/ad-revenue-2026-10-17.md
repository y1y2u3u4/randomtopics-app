## Diagnosis
1. **A quarter of public page views carry no ad code**, so no anchor or vignette (the two formats that earn 78%) can show there.
2. **The manual in-page unit sits under the FAQ**: 15% viewable, $0.25 impression RPM.
3. **All ads are non-personalized with restricted data processing** (`requestNonPersonalizedAds = 1`, `data-privacy-treatments="disablePersonalization"`, RDP) for every visitor, including US visitors who have not opted out. Non-personalized ads usually pay noticeably less. Changing this is a privacy-policy decision → left to the user (see below).
4. ~1.6 views per session: "Keep playing" links sit at the very bottom of tool pages.
