## Measurement change on 2026-10-11 (~02:15 UTC)

To fix homepage mobile LCP, the CMP + AdSense scripts (PR #96) and gtag.js +
the usage script (PR #97) now load after window load + idle, the first
interaction, or 4 s. `gtag()` still queues events from the first script, so
events are kept unless a visitor leaves before the page finishes loading. When
comparing the 7-day and 14-day windows, treat 2026-10-11 as a partial day and
watch sessions/active users for a step change before reading the rates.

## Measurement change on 2026-10-12 (01:24 UTC, PR #101) — restart the baseline here

PR #97 lost about a third of GA sessions on 10/11 (sessions −39% vs 10/4 while
AdSense page views rose): short visits ended before gtag loaded. gtag.js and the
usage script now load at the first idle moment, at most 1.5 s after the page
starts (ads/CMP stay deferred as in #96). Mobile PSI on `/` after the change:
LCP median 3.30 s over 5 runs (5.99, 3.30, 3.30, 3.38, 3.15). Treat 10/11 and
10/12 as unusable for the motion A/B and start the comparison windows on
2026-10-13.
