displayed daily or random question belongs to Funny & Light, offer the existing
independent 100-question funny collection immediately after the primary actions.

daily date, random result or saved identity transfers to the destination. Its
existing filters select the destination's actual corpus. General, student,
work and funny collections retain their existing canonical owners. No titles,
metadata, corpus, routes, index policy or list-copy controls change.
